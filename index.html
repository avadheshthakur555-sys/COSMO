📖 COSMO'S JOURNEY — EPISODE I
Escape from the Sun! ☀️
COSMO was a tiny energy being born inside the Sun's roaring core 🔥

One day a massive solar flare launched COSMO into the vast cosmos — tumbling, bouncing and soaring through the Solar System!

From the scorching Sun, through cratered Mercury, toxic Venus, beautiful Earth Orbit, rust-red Mars, stormy Jupiter and ringed Saturn…

Riding a cosmic portal, COSMO then discovers Planet Earth — the savannas of Africa 🦁, castles of Europe 🏰, dragons of Asia 🐉, eagles of the Americas 🦅, reefs of Oceania 🦘, ice of Antarctica 🐧, and auroras of the Arctic 🌌

Collect gems 💎, dodge hazards ⚠️, find portals 🌀 — the cosmos is yours, pilot!
